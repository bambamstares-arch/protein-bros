Top 5 Protein Powders of 2026: Whey, Isolate & Plant, Ranked by the Science

Protein Bros Research DeskUpdated June 24, 202611 min read

The protein powder aisle is a marketing warzone. Strip away the hype and only three things determine whether a powder builds muscle: total protein per dollar, leucine content (the amino acid that triggers muscle protein synthesis), and whether an independent lab has verified what's actually in the tub.

The evidence here is unusually settled. Meta-analyses show that once you hit roughly 1.6 g of protein per kg of bodyweight per day, the source matters far less than the total — but higher-leucine, higher-digestibility proteins (measured by the DIAAS score) make hitting that target easier.

The Rankings
1
Optimum Nutrition
Gold Standard 100% Whey

The reference standard. 24g protein and ~2.7g of leucine per scoop — comfortably above the ~2-3g leucine threshold that maximally stimulates muscle protein synthesis.
